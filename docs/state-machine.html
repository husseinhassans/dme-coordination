DME coordination engine

Design for the take-home, version 2 after a full audit. One engine runs a Medicare equipment case from "intake done" to "chair delivered and billed right." It calls suppliers, the doctor's office and the patient on its own, and hands the case to a person only for ten named reasons. Everything below follows one simulated run of Eleanor Martinez's case. It hits all five failure modes in the brief. Four are recovered automatically, and one is handed to a person on purpose. Drag the timeline to replay it.

Design decisions a reviewer could challenge

#DecisionWhy
D1Assume intake captured a HIPAA authorization naming the company and consent to automated calls. The engine checks both before starting.The office won't talk to us without it. AI voice calls need consent.
D2Every call opens by saying it's an automated assistant.Disclosure and trust with an older patient.
D3Scouting calls never name her. Her name, MBI and order go only to the committed supplier, and only after she consents. Voicemails carry no health details.HIPAA minimum necessary.
D4The engine prefills the order for Dr. Chen to sign and sends the public coverage checklist with it.Allowed for non-power items. Prevents the documentation gap behind 79.7% of improper payments.
D5The signed order comes to the engine, which validates it and relays it to the supplier.Catches errors before the supplier does. The order names no supplier, so it moves to a replacement unchanged.
D6Delivery waits for a validated order, even though K0001 doesn't require it.A wrong code on a delivered chair makes it unbillable.
D7The validator checks the six order elements, K0001, quantity 1, and that the description matches the code. "Order dated after the visit" is our own sanity check.Labeled so nobody mistakes our check for a Medicare rule.
D8The LLM flags criteria the note never mentions, with quotes. It doesn't judge coverage and never writes clinical text.A false flag costs one polite request.
D9Supplier cutoffs (ETA ≤ 10 days, restock ≤ 14) are product policy in config.Not Medicare rules.
D10Eleanor approves the supplier before anything is scheduled.Beneficiaries choose their supplier.
D11"Delivered" needs the supplier's proof of delivery and her confirmation.Two independent signals. A mismatch goes to a person.
D12Every LLM fact needs a verbatim quote that the rules find by string match. No quote means unknown. No confidence scores.Checkable. Self-reported LLM confidence isn't calibrated.
D13Patient calls 9:00–18:00, at her best time, one routine call a day, plus one urgent call when something slips.Call fatigue drives the disengagement failure mode.
D14One calendar for every timer: Mon–Fri without federal holidays.The run crosses Veterans Day and handles it.
D15The intro call asks about a wheelchair after her March knee surgery.A prior rental silently kills the claim. Asking costs one question.

Verified facts the design rests on

#FactSource
F1K0001 for home use needs coverage criteria A–E plus F or G. "Difficulty walking" doesn't meet them. A chair only for use outside the home isn't covered.DME MAC letter, LCD L33788
F2Insufficient documentation causes 79.7% of improper payments on manual wheelchairs.CMS MLN
F3An order needs six elements: name or MBI, description, quantity, practitioner name or NPI, date, signature. It names no supplier.CMS order requirements
F4Someone else may fill in the order for non-power items. The practitioner signs.A55426
F5K0001 isn't on the order-before-delivery list. K0002–K0007 are, effective Oct 28, 2026.CMS list
F6K0001 is a capped rental for up to 13 months. Months 4–13 pay 75% of the fee.CMS fee schedule notes
F760614 is in the former Chicago bidding area. The K0001 rental allowance there is $34.27 a month.CMS DME26-D files
F8Competitive bidding is paused until 2028, so any enrolled supplier can provide the chair.CMS Round 2028
F9A supplier that doesn't accept assignment can charge her more. No limiting charge applies to DME.CMS letter
F10The 2026 Part B deductible is $283. It resets every January.2026 amounts
F11A new rental period needs a 60-day break in need. Prior rentals show up as "same or similar."Noridian
F12QMB pays all Medicare cost-sharing. Illinois 2026 limits are about $1,330 a month in income and $9,950 in assets. Suppliers can't bill QMB enrollees.IL Dept. on Aging
